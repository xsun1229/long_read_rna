We compared all 22 autosomes, four donors and three timepoints (264 runs) against the donor genotype panel. Among 35,829 adjudicable calls, 96.0% have a confirmed alternate allele and 69.1% have an exactly matching genotype. The main discrepancy is a panel heterozygote called homozygous-alternate; its frequency and the cost of increasing the depth cutoff are shown below.

What was compared?

  • isoLASER: completed default --platform Nanopore runs (ont_roc), for CD_21, CD_31, CD_36 and CD_48 at 0, 1 and 6 hours. These are the GM12878 ONT ROC model results, separate from the GTEx-model runs.
  • Reference: the 100-line TOPMed-imputed genotype panel, using the corresponding four donor genotypes and GRCh38 coordinates.
  • Included calls: PASS single-nucleotide variants with variant-site depth DP ≥ 10, one concrete alternate allele and a callable diploid genotype. The gVCF <NON_REF> placeholder is ignored.
  • Counting unit: one donor × timepoint × variant. Repeated gene-level records for that call are collapsed by retaining the deepest record. Observations of the same variant in different donors or timepoints are counted separately.

The 79,752 calls represent 22,128 distinct genomic variants. They are variant calls, not splicing associations.

Panel VCF: /project/xinhe/lifan/neuron_stim/zicheng_100lines_all_snp_sorted.vcf.gz.

Matching: panel records are located by chromosome and position, then matching reference/alternate alleles are preferred. If there is no allele-matched record, a unique panel record at that position can still document an allele discrepancy. Conflicting panel records are marked ambiguous and excluded from adjudication; they do not expand the number of calls. This affects 0 calls.

How much does the panel confirm?

An adjudicable call has an unambiguous, callable donor panel genotype and site-level imputation R² ≥ 0.8. This is the denominator for both agreement rates.

Alternate-allele confirmation requires matching reference and alternate alleles and a non-reference panel genotype. Exact genotype agreement also requires the same number of alternate alleles, ignoring phasing orientation. For example, panel 0/1 and isoLASER 1/1 confirm the alternate allele but are not an exact genotype match.

Among adjudicable calls, 1,416 have a homozygous-reference panel genotype and 25 have different alleles. These categories can overlap; neither contributes to alternate-allele confirmation.

Main discrepancy: heterozygotes called homozygous

Among allele-matched, adjudicable calls with a heterozygous panel genotype, 9,423 / 22,626 (41.6%) are called homozygous-alternate by isoLASER.

Genotype discrepancy by variant-site read depth, across all autosomes and samples.

Here DP is the number of reads overlapping the variant position in the isoLASER gVCF. It is different from the informative-read count for a particular variant–exonic-part association used in the allele-specific splicing screen. Thus, a variant-site DP cutoff and a splicing-association read cutoff address different quantities.

The discrepant calls have a median reported alternate-read fraction (AB) of 0.58. A homozygous call therefore does not necessarily mean that no reference-allele reads were observed.

Increasing DP trades coverage for agreement in the retained subset. It cannot recover heterozygotes missed by the original caller or restore their contribution to phasing. These repeated donor/timepoint observations are descriptive counts, not independent biological replicates.

Interpretation and reproducibility

This comparison measures agreement among variants isoLASER called. It does not estimate sensitivity, since panel variants missed by isoLASER are not counted, and the imputed panel is not an error-free truth set. Alternate-allele confirmation and exact genotype agreement should both be reported because phasing depends on recognizing heterozygous sites.

The results were regenerated from all 264 gVCFs using 8.isolaser_validation/19.validate_all_autosomes.py; summaries and figures use 8.isolaser_validation/20.all_autosome_assets.R. The earlier chr1/chr10-only assets are preserved separately.
