How we selected the tissue

We chose the brain tissue with the most isoform-ratio QTLs, since SCZ is a brain trait. The counts are Pantry’s conditionally independent cis-QTLs per GTEx v8 tissue (gtex.separate_kdp.qtls.tsv.gz, downloaded 2026-10-29).

Brain Cerebellum has the most among brain tissues: 6,755 isoform-ratio QTLs in 6,067 isoforms of 4,998 genes. Testis has the most overall. Pantry codes the two cerebellum tissues BRNCHA and BRNCHB. Its covariate files have 209 samples for BRNCHA and 175 for BRNCHB, the GTEx v8 sample sizes of Brain Cerebellum and Cerebellar Hemisphere, so BRNCHA is Brain Cerebellum.

How the runs were set up

  • GWAS: PGC schizophrenia, ieu-b-5102 (N = 127,906), z-scores harmonized to the UK Biobank (UKB) LD reference, as for the M-cTWAS paper.
  • eQTL and sQTL weights: the paper’s GTEx v8 PredictDB expression and splicing models for Brain Cerebellum.
  • Isoform-ratio weights (our definition): one trait per isoform, from Pantry’s independent cis-QTLs in Brain Cerebellum. They come from the genes significant in Pantry’s permutation test, q < 0.05.
    • Each isoform’s QTL is its strongest independent variant (smallest nominal p) among single-base variants in the UKB reference that are not strand-ambiguous.
    • The weight is that variant’s effect on the isoform ratio (tensorQTL’s slope); with one SNP per trait, only its sign matters.
    • Genes come from Pantry’s own annotation. We used top QTLs, not Munro’s prediction models.
  • Settings, as the paper’s runs: each QTL type is its own group, with protein-coding genes only and LD from the UKB reference. eQTL and sQTL weights are scaled (scale_predictdb_weights), the isoform weights are not (one SNP per trait). No thinning, one prior variance shared by all groups (shared_all), up to 5 signals per region (L = 5).
  • Not done yet: merging regions and checking LD mismatch.

What the tables and figures show

Parameters, the output of summarize_param():

  • group_size: molecular traits that reach cTWAS after matching to the GWAS and the LD reference.
  • group_prior: the estimated prior probability that a trait (or a SNP) is causal.
  • group_prior_var: the prior variance of a causal effect, shared by all groups (shared_all).
  • log_enrichment: the log of a group’s prior over the SNP prior, with its standard error; enrichment_pval is its p-value.
  • group_pve: the proportion of SCZ variance explained by the group; total_pve sums all groups and SNPs.
  • prop_heritability: a group’s group_pve over total_pve, the slices of the pie charts.

Convergence, from make_convergence_plots(): the estimates at each iteration of the parameter estimation (proportion causal π is group_prior, effect size σ2 is group_prior_var, enrichment is πG/πS, and PVE).

Gene-level PIPs: combined_pip combines all of a gene’s molecular traits and QTL types with combine_gene_pips() (by = "type", method = "combine_cs", filter_cs = FALSE). eQTL_pip, sQTL_pip and isoQTL_pip are the gene’s PIP from each QTL type; “–” means the gene has no trait of that type in a fine-mapped region. The mapping table assigns each trait to its gene: the paper’s PredictDB mapping for eQTL genes and sQTL introns, and Pantry’s annotation for isoforms.

Convergence

eQTL + sQTL converged in 17 iterations. eQTL + sQTL + isoform ratio stopped at 50 iterations without converging: the isoQTL prior was still decreasing towards zero while the eQTL, sQTL and SNP estimates had settled, so its estimate is an upper bound. Both runs fine-mapped 204 regions.
