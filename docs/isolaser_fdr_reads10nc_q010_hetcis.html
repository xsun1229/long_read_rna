This page reports read-based isoLASER without the clustering filter at the cutoffs chosen on 2026-10-28: BH q < 0.10 and |ΔPSI| ≥ 0.05, with isoLASER’s cis label. The earlier page used q < 0.05 and |ΔPSI| ≥ 0.10. Everything else is unchanged and described there: the run, the donors and family rules, the permutation test and BH within each timepoint. The q-values themselves are the same. BH was computed over the full family of tested associations in each timepoint, before the cis / ΔPSI screen, so the new cutoffs only report more of them. The FDR target is now 10% within each timepoint’s full family. The effect-filtered subset and the union across timepoints have no separate guarantee.

Main result: 67 associations in 30 genes pass, against 45 in 22 genes at the earlier cutoffs. None are lost. The 8 new genes enter through the lower q cutoff alone (ASRGL1, DGCR6L, EIF6, PATJ, SDHA, TMEM9); the lower |ΔPSI| cutoff alone (C11orf58); both changes together (NME2).

Genes to follow up

We apply the same two conditions as the earlier page (our definitions):

  1. Reported only after stimulation: at 1 or 6 hours, not at 0 hours.
  2. Neuron- or stimulation-related: a neuronal GO term, a disorder in SFARI or DDG2P, or a change in expression or splicing after stimulation in our data.
