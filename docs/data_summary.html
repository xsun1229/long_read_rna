The dataset

Long-read (Oxford Nanopore) single-nucleus RNA-seq of hiPSC-derived neurons from the Duan lab — the same cells and donors as Liang et al., Science 392:eadw3949 (2026), assayed on Nanopore rather than the published 10x Multiome. Not itself published.

data/NP184_NP187_NP188_combined_DUAN_21-0/   0 hr   27.5 GB
data/NP189_NP190_NP193_combined_DUAN_21-1/   1 hr   34.2 GB
data/NP191_NP192_NP194_combined_DUAN_21-6/   6 hr   36.4 GB

Coculture batch 21, four donors, three KCl-stimulation timepoints (0 / 1 / 6 h). The three NP identifiers in each name are sequencing runs (flow cells) of that one library — three per library, all distinct across the three files.

Two scNanoGPS conventions differ from what single-cell tooling expects:

  • the cell barcode is in the RG tag, not CB
  • the UMI is appended to the read name, not in UB
QNAME  281b9c59-a0da-477b-84b9-d9895f6ec585_TTCAAGATATAT
RG:Z:  TTCGTTAGTACCAGGT

This is convenient for one thing: samtools view -R <barcode_list> splits a BAM by donor directly, with no re-tagging.

The constraint that matters most: intronic dominance

Read midpoints against the IsoQuant annotation, chr21 (0 hr):

Ours (chr21) scNanoGPS, genome-wide [slides]
Exonic 19.5% 11.6%
Intronic 74.2% 68.4%
Intergenic 6.3% 20.0%

This is single-nucleus RNA, so the libraries are dominated by unspliced nuclear pre-mRNA. Reads inside introns cannot be assigned to a mature transcript, which is what exon-centric isoform methods require.

It directly explains the largest loss in the isoLASER pilot (unassigned_tx_id = 53.8% of reads, overall retention 14.8%). That is inherent to the assay, not a tunable parameter. It is consistent across timepoints (% spliced 19.5–20.2%).

Background to filter

  • rRNA carryover — 677 k of chr21’s 1.31 M primary reads lie in the chr21:8.2–8.6 Mb rDNA array; scaffolds GL000220.1 and KI270733.1 are also heavily loaded.
  • Mitochondrial — 6.3–7.5% of primary reads.
  • Rat astrocytes were in the coculture [metadata]. They are filtered out of the metadata file, but rat-derived reads may still be in the BAMs.

Stimulation worked

Immediate-early genes at 0 hr are low, with no activated population — the expected baseline:

IEG % of cells (0 hr) IEG % of cells (0 hr)
BDNF 16.1 NR4A1 2.8
EGR1 12.6 FOSB 1.2
FOS 9.6 ARC 0.9
JUNB 9.6 NPAS4 0.3

The published design has ERGs (FOS, NPAS4) peaking at 1 h and LRGs (BDNF, VGF) at 6 h, so these should rise sharply in the 1 hr and 6 hr libraries. Not yet measured on the new files — worth doing as a positive control that the timepoints are what they claim.

What is now possible

Differential splicing across stimulation — 0 / 1 / 6 h for the same four donors, paired within donor, so donor genetics is controlled by construction. Given the intronic dominance, junction-level intron clustering (LeafCutter-style) is likely better powered here than isoform-level methods.

Allele-specific splicing — four donors with barcode-level assignment, so no genotype demultiplexing is needed. isolaser_joint can run over 12 samples (4 donors × 3 timepoints), or the timepoints can be pooled per donor for maximum depth since haplotypes do not change with stimulation. The chr21 test run is here.

Caveat on scale: four donors is small for anything genetic — the paper used 100 lines for its QTL mapping. What is realistically deliverable is allele-specific splicing within donors plus cross-donor consistency.

Practical traps

cell_barcodes_w_meta.txt.gz is not line-parseable. The cell.type.counts column is a plot label containing a literal newline, so every record spans two lines and the barcode sits on the second. wc -l reports 46,495 lines for 23,247 records, and grep 21-1 returns rows without barcodes. Parse by re-pairing:

zcat cell_barcodes_w_meta.txt.gz | awk -F'\t' '
  NF==15 { lib=$1; time=$4; line=$6; type=$13; next }
  NF==2  { print $2"\t"lib"\t"time"\t"line"\t"type }'

Do not use samtools fastq on these BAMs. With ~20% of reads carrying QUAL='*', mixed input makes it truncate silently — 629,035 reads in, 14,255 out, no error. Each half converts correctly in isolation, so it is the interleaving. Convert explicitly and assert the read count.

Open questions

  1. Can we get the cell-line assignment before the cell-type filter (5,035 / 8,097 / 6,911 rather than 4,866 / 7,891 / 6,625)?
  2. At what stage were the base qualities lost for 16–21% of reads?
  3. Is there documentation for these BAMs? They carry no sample-level annotation — @RG holds only a bare 16 bp barcode, no SM/LB/DS.
  4. Are there long-read data for other coculture batches beyond batch 21? Four donors limits the genetic analyses.
